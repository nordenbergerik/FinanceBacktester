REFERENCE / STRATEGY RULES

Available indicators

These are the values you can use in entry and exit conditions. They are different from the performance metrics shown after a backtest.

6 TYPES
PRICE

Adjusted close

Compare the asset's adjusted closing price against a value or moving average.

SMA

Simple moving average

Average price over a rolling window.

Parameter: period
EMA

Exponential moving average

Moving average with more weight on recent prices.

Parameter: period
RSI

Relative strength index

Momentum oscillator commonly interpreted on a 0–100 scale.

Parameter: period
MACD

Moving average convergence/divergence

Compare the MACD line, signal line, or histogram.

Parameters: fast_period, slow_period, signal_period, component
BOLLINGER

Bollinger bands

Compare price with the upper, middle, or lower band.

Indicators: BOLLINGER_UPPER, BOLLINGER_MIDDLE, BOLLINGER_LOWER · Parameters: period, num_std_dev
